Mission


Pawsitive RescYou is a project dedicated to facilitating the search and identification of abandoned animals in Puerto Rico. According to the Sato Project, there are around 650,000 abandoned animals in Puerto Rico, and it is recognized that helping and rescuing them is a challenging task. Our project aims to address this issue by connecting with rescuers and collaborating with various shelters to give these animals a second chance.
We strive to contribute to the change these animals need.

Remember, let’s stay Pawsitive and bark to the world!

Our

History



Pawsitive RescYou is a project created by college student Dianeris Jimenez, who has a deep love and appreciation for animals. Since she was a little girl, she has wanted to make an impact and always envisioned herself helping animals. This project was created because Dianeris adopted a rescue animal and has maintained a good relationship with the rescuer of her adopted animal ever since.

Through conversations and analysis with rescuers, she realized that animal shelters and rescuers are not always able to help animals abandoned on the street, either due to economic difficulties or lack of space to provide them with a good quality of life. In response to this concern, Dianeris wanted to help rescuers by providing them with an application that allows them to reach the animals directly and see how they can help.

This project aims to change not only the lives of the animals but also to simplify the work of our rescuers, who carry out this devotion with much love.
