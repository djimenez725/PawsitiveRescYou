Pawsitive RescYou App

Pawsitive RescYou was created with the aim to help abandoned animals and facilitate their location through our app. The app has a clear and simple purpose: if someone wants to report an abandoned animal, the app will display the nearest rescuers and shelters based on their location. If the rescuer cannot arrive immediately, the person will receive instructions on how to care for the animal until the rescuer arrives. Additionally, we have partnerships with shelters to take in the animals and provide them with the necessary care. This ensures a second chance for the animals, helping them find suitable homes.
