Let’s stay Pawsitive!




Pawsitive RescYou is an initiative devoted to help decrease the amount of abandoned animals in Puerto Rico. Through partnerships with rescuers and shelters, we have helped many animals in need.





Pawsitive

RescYou

App



The purpose of this app is to report abandoned animals so that the nearest animal rescuer or shelter can help them. Once someone reports an abandoned animal, the app displays the nearest shelters and rescuers based on the person's location. If a rescuer cannot arrive immediately, you will be given instructions on how to care for the animal until help arrives.
