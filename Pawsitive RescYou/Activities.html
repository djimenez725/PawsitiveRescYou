RescYou Day!

Rescuers and shelters make this project a reality. On this day, we carry out various activities to honor our rescuers. This activitie is about appreciation and respect for the admiriable work they do daily. Also, our rescuers can see the rescued animals living a happy life, experiencing the true satisfaction of witnessing these rescued animals living their best lives.

Paws and Whiskers: Let's Run!

Let’s dedicate a day to the joyful chaos of owning our small animals! This activity promotes adoption while celebrating the happiness and beauty of giving second chances. You’ll see our efforts and how these families thrive with their new additions. Get ready for some fun with dogs running around and enjoying themselves in the green space, showcasing their beautiful personalities.
